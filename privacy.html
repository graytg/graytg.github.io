rclone-backup privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Who uses it

rclone-backup is used only by its owner, to back up their own files. It has no other users.

What it can access

It asks Google for the narrowest Drive access there is (drive.file): it can see and change only the files it creates itself in the owner's Google Drive. It can't see any other files in that Drive.

What happens to the data

  • The backed-up files go from the owner's server to the owner's Google Drive, and nowhere else.
  • No personal data about anyone is collected, sold, or shared.
  • The sign-in token Google issues is kept on the owner's own server.
